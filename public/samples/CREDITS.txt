Bass Chord Lab — instrument samples

The files in this folder are short excerpts of the libraries below, trimmed, level-matched
and encoded as MP3 by tools/build-samples.py.

piano/            Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5), by Alexander Holm.
                  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0),
                  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
                  Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
                  Changes: two of the sixteen dynamics, eight notes from E-flat 3 up, 4.5 seconds each.

drums/            Virtuosity Drums, by Versilian Studios, played by Austin McMahon.
                  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain).
                  Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
                  Changes: overhead microphones, with the close microphone mixed in on
                  kick, snare and cross-stick.

guitar-acoustic/  University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (Electronic Music Studios).
                  "May be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."
                  Source: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
                  via https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

guitar-electric/  Karoryfer Samples. Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain).
                  Source: https://www.karoryfer.com/karoryfer-samples
                  via https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
